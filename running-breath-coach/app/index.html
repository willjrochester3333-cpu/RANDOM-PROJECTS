Rhythm strength by breathing rate. The marked peak is your breathing; peaks to its left are the same rhythm counted every second or third breath.

Breath sound test

Follow the circle for 45 seconds: breathe in while it grows, out while it shrinks, loudly through your mouth. BreathPace checks whether it hears one sound per breath or two (in and out), and sets itself up to count correctly.
