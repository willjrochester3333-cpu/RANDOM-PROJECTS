        Set up a radio (UK settings)

        Do this once for each of the two radios, before the rocket's radio goes into the rocket: plug ONE radio into this computer's USB (nothing else needs to be on), press Set up and choose its port. The page reads all its settings, sets the transmit power to 8 dBm (6.3 mW: with the 2 dBi antennas that is about 6 mW e.r.p., inside the UK's 10 mW limit for 433.05–434.79 MHz) and the network ID to 75, so both radios match and ignore other SiK users, saves and restarts the radio. Everything else stays at the SiK defaults (57600 baud, 64 kbit/s air rate, MAVLink framing on).
