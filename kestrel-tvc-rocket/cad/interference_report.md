# Interference check

55 solids (1485 pairs) checked with exact mesh booleans.

**No overlaps** (> 1 mm^3) between any printed part and any purchased part.

## Gimbal sweep

Motor, holder, retainer and ring swung round the whole deflection circle (24 directions at 7 deg = firmware limit 6.0 deg + 1 deg margin): 550 contact checks.

## Control-tab sweep

All 4 tabs with their horns, pushrods and servo horns swept through +/-17 deg (limit 15 deg + 2 deg): 324 contact checks.

## Payload-door sweep

Door, latch knob and cam (turned to open) swung 0-100 deg about the hinge: 132 contact checks.

## Release latch

Bolt engages the nose sleeve by 1.05 mm when locked and clears it by 2.45 mm when pulled in 3.5 mm; released bolt and horn checked against the insert, nose and servo: 5 checks.

**No contact** anywhere in any mechanism's travel.
