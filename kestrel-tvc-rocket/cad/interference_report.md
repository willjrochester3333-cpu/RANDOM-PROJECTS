# Interference check

52 solids (1326 pairs) checked with exact mesh booleans.

**No overlaps** (> 1 mm^3) between any printed part and any purchased part.

## Gimbal sweep

Motor, holder, both retainers, the 24 mm adapter sleeve and the ring swung round the whole deflection circle (24 directions at 7 deg = firmware limit 6.0 deg + 1 deg margin) against the frame, fin can, tube, servos, horns and pushrods: 1350 contact checks.

## 24 mm adapter

Adapter sleeve, 24 mm retainer, D-motor spacer, a 24 x 95 mm (E) and a 24 x 70 mm (D) motor checked against each other and the holder: 9 checks; the D motor + spacer stack is 0.7 mm short of the washer ledge.

## Control-tab sweep

All 4 tabs with their glued-in servo horns swept through +/-17 deg (limit 15 deg + 2 deg): 216 contact checks.

## Payload-door sweep

Door, latch knob and cam (turned to open) swung 0-100 deg about the hinge: 132 contact checks.

## Release latch

Bolt engages the nose sleeve by 1.05 mm when locked and clears it by 2.45 mm when pulled in 3.5 mm; released bolt and horn checked against the insert, nose and servo: 5 checks.

**No contact** anywhere in any mechanism's travel.
